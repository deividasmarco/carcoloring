Neon Bay

Privacy policy

Published by Atiduok · Effective 15 October 2026

In short

  • Neon Bay has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The pictures children paint stay on the device. We never receive them.
  • The only service the app contacts is RevenueCat, which checks whether a subscription is active. It uses a random ID that does not identify anyone.

Who we are

Neon Bay is a car colouring app for children, published by Atiduok (“we”). For any question about privacy, email deividasmar@gmail.com.

What stays on the device

Neon Bay keeps these in its own storage on the device:

  • the pictures painted in the app (the brush strokes and preview images);
  • settings: whether visual effects and vibration are on, and which car was last chosen in the garage.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting a picture in My Creations removes it, and deleting the app removes everything. If the device owner has turned on device backups (such as iCloud Backup or Google backup), these files can be included in those backups, which Apple or Google keep in the owner’s account. We have no access to them.

Saving and sharing pictures

  • Save puts a copy of the picture in the device’s photo library. The first time, the device asks for permission to add photos. Neon Bay only adds its own pictures; it does not look at or upload other photos.
  • Share opens the device’s own share options. Whether a picture is shared, and with whom, is chosen on the device. Neon Bay does not send the picture anywhere itself.

Subscriptions

Some vehicles are part of a subscription. It can only be bought, restored or managed in the parent area, behind a parental gate.

  • Payment is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies (Apple, Google). We never see payment details, names or email addresses.
  • To check the subscription, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. (United States), which processes this data for us:
    • a random app user ID that the app creates on first use, not linked to a name, an email address or an advertising ID;
    • the purchase receipt or token from the store: which subscription it is, and when it was bought, renewed or ended;
    • basic technical details: device type, operating system and app version, and the store’s country and currency.
  • The app checks the subscription with RevenueCat when it starts and when it comes back to the screen, including when nothing has been bought.
  • We use this data to unlock the subscription vehicles, restore the subscription after reinstalling the app or on another device, prevent fraudulent purchases, and see subscription totals in RevenueCat’s reports (for example, how many subscriptions are active). It is not used for advertising or to track anyone across other apps or websites.
  • Neon Bay turns off RevenueCat’s collection of device identifiers and its diagnostics, and does not use the device’s advertising identifier.
  • RevenueCat stores this data in the United States. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Links outside the app

Links to websites (such as this policy) and to the store’s subscription settings appear only in the parent area, behind the parental gate.

What Neon Bay does not do

  • no accounts or sign-in;
  • no ads, and no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools;
  • no chat or other ways to contact people;
  • no use of location, the camera, the microphone or contacts;
  • we do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

Statistics from Apple and Google

If the device owner has chosen in the device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple or Google may give us summary statistics, such as crash counts and the number of installs. These do not identify anyone, and the device settings control them.

Children

Neon Bay is made for children and is designed not to collect their personal information. The random ID and purchase receipt described above are used only to provide the subscription, which a parent buys. If you think we hold information about your child, contact us and we will delete it.

Legal basis (EU and UK)

We process subscription data to provide the subscription that was bought (performance of a contract), and to prevent fraud and see subscription totals (our legitimate interests).

How long data is kept

Pictures and settings stay on the device until they are deleted. Subscription records are kept for as long as they are needed to provide the subscription, and after that only as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the subscription data, to correct or delete it, or to stop using it. Email us with the purchase date and the order ID from your App Store or Google Play receipt, so we can find the record. Deleting the record does not cancel the subscription: cancel it in the App Store or Google Play subscription settings. In the EU and the UK you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
